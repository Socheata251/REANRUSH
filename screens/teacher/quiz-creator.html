lightbulb Battle Explanation (Shown to students in post-question review) Bilingual Note

By Ampere's Law for a solenoid, B = μ₀ · (N / L) · I. If current (I) and length (L) stay constant, magnetic flux density (B) is directly proportional to turns (N). Doubling N doubles B.
